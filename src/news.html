New ARDaC Portal Release Now Available

May 2026

The ARDaC Portal has been updated with new data resources and features to support addiction research and data discovery.

This release includes the addition of the TREAT study, newly available proteomics data, and the new Anagine Summary Report feature.

The Anagine Summary Report allows users to generate a one-click PDF report from filtered Explorer data, including cohort-level summaries, data availability, participant and visit characteristics, biospecimen and lab result summaries, and visual outputs where available.

To help users become familiar with the new portal, ARDaC will host a user training session in mid-June 2026. The training will introduce the new portal, TREAT study data, proteomics resources, and the Anagine Summary Report feature.

Previous training materials, including slides, recordings, and user guides, are available here: Training Slides (PPTX) and Training Recording (YouTube).

During the transition period, the previous portal version will remain temporarily available through an archive site. More information about the transition timeline will be provided separately.

For questions or support, please contact the ARDaC team at ardac@iu.edu.
